## 🧩 Summary

<p style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
"Each day, after the first execution vote is tallied, you may publicly choose a player who voted: they die."
</p>

<p style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
The <strong>Gunslinger</strong> shoots players who voted during the first execution of the day.
</p>

<ul style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
  <li>Once per day, after the first execution vote, the Gunslinger may <strong>publicly choose a voting player</strong> to die instantly.</li>
  <li>The ability is optional.</li>
  <li>Only one shot per day.</li>
  <li><strong>Exiles</strong> are not affected — the Gunslinger cannot shoot after exile votes.</li>
</ul>

---

## ⚙️ How it works

<p style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
After the first execution vote of the day, when the vote count is announced,  
the Gunslinger may declare they are using their ability and point at one of the players who voted.  
That player <strong>dies immediately</strong>.  
The Storyteller can ask all voters to raise their hands again if clarification is needed.
</p>

---

## 💡 Examples

<ul style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
  <li>The <span style="color:#d45b5b;">[Imp](../tb_roles/imp.md)</span> is nominated.  
  → Five out of ten players vote.  
  → The Gunslinger chooses one of them: that player dies immediately, but the Imp remains on the block.</li>

  <li>The <a href="../tb_roles/voleur.md" style="color:#4ea3ff; font-weight:bold; text-decoration:none;">Thief</a> is exiled.  
  → Later, the <span style="color:#d45b5b;">[Butler](../tb_roles/majordome.md)</span> is nominated and receives one vote.  
  → It’s the first vote of the day, so the Gunslinger chooses to shoot the voter.</li>
</ul>

---

## 🧿 Tips & Tricks (if you are <span style="color:#4ea3ff;">Good</span>)

<ul style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
  <li>Kills caused by your ability are <strong>not executions</strong>: they don’t end the day and are not seen by the <a href="../tb_roles/croquemort.md" style="color:#4ea3ff; font-weight:bold; text-decoration:none;">Undertaker</a>.</li>
  <li>Using your power every day doubles the Good team’s chances of killing the <span style="color:#d45b5b;">Demon</span>.</li>
  <li>Watch who doesn’t vote — <span style="color:#d45b5b;">Demons</span> and <span style="color:#d45b5b;">Minions</span> often hesitate to raise their hand in fear of you.</li>
  <li>You are the perfect counter to the <a href="../tb_roles/saint.md" style="color:#4ea3ff; font-weight:bold; text-decoration:none;">Saint</a>: they can’t be executed, but you can shoot them.</li>
  <li>Be careful near the end: with only three players left, a wrong shot might give victory to Evil.</li>
</ul>

---

## 🔥 Tips & Tricks (if you are <span style="color:#d45b5b;">Evil</span>)

<ul style="color:#e0c99d; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
  <li>Like a Good Gunslinger, shoot daily — but aim wisely.</li>
  <li>Avoid killing your own <span style="color:#d45b5b;">Demon</span> (unless a <a href="../tb_roles/femmeecarlate.md" style="color:#d45b5b; font-weight:bold; text-decoration:none;">Scarlet Woman</a> can benefit).</li>
  <li>Eliminate strong Good roles such as the <a href="../tb_roles/voyante.md" style="color:#4ea3ff; font-weight:bold; text-decoration:none;">Fortune Teller</a> or the <a href="../tb_roles/soldat.md" style="color:#4ea3ff; font-weight:bold; text-decoration:none;">Soldier</a> early.</li>
  <li>Use fear to your advantage: threaten to shoot to control voting behavior.</li>
  <li>In the final days, with only three players alive, shoot a non-Demon and let chaos decide the outcome.</li>
</ul>
